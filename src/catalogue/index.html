Playground Samples Docs

Every abap2UI5 sample, in one place

Three repositories, one catalogue. Learn is the path through abap2UI5 itself — bindings, events, popups, navigation, whole apps. Controls is the UI5 demo kit rebuilt in ABAP, one port per sample. Stack is abap2UI5 with OData, RAP, WebSockets or the launchpad.

Search by what a sample does, or filter by the control it builds, the library it needs, or the UI5 release your system runs. Most of them open in the playground and run in this browser with nothing installed; the ones that need a real system say so, and open for reading instead.

The catalogue is a list of 700-odd samples and needs JavaScript to filter. Without it, the three repositories are readable on GitHub: samples, samples-controls, samples-stack.

Built from the catalogues the three repositories commit — abap2UI5/samples, samples-controls, samples-stack — and rebuilt on every deploy of the playground. Every sample also has a page of its own — the plain list, for reading down and for linking to.
